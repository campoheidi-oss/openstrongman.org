About

What this project is, why it exists, and how to get involved.

What is OpenStrongman?

OpenStrongman is a free, open archive of strongman competition results across all major US federations. Results are logged exactly as competed (weights, times, distances, reps) and organized using a consistent biomechanical event category system so data are useful across different federations and formats.

The project is built in the spirit of open data. All results are downloadable as CSV, freely usable for research, coaching analysis, or building your own tools. No account, no paywall, no ads, ever.

Why does this exist?

Results from thousands of strongman competitions exist only as PDFs, Facebook posts, and scattered spreadsheets. Athletes have no reliable way to track their history across federations. Coaches can't benchmark athletes objectively. Researchers have no dataset to work with.

OpenStrongman exists to fix that. We're building a permanent, community-maintained record of the sport. Not affiliated with any federation or existing platforms, not trying to replace any federation or existing platforms, just making the historical record accessible.

How is data collected?

Data are researched from publicly available sources; including official results pages, federation websites, and competition records. Then entered manually following our open schema. We prioritize accuracy over speed: every result is verified before it's published.

We are actively working toward data partnerships with federations and platforms to make collection more efficient over time. Results are stored as open CSV files in a public GitHub repository, giving the data a permanent home and full version history.

☕

OpenStrongman is free, and will stay free.

No subscriptions, no ads, no paywalls - ever. The data belongs to the community. If you find this project useful and want to support the work that goes into maintaining it, you're welcome to buy us a coffee. It's entirely optional and always appreciated.

Contribute data

Have results we're missing? Know a federation or meet that should be included? We'd love your help. OpenStrongman grows through community contributions - whether that's submitting a single meet result or helping us build a partnership with a federation.

Open an issue on our GitHub repository and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Affiliations

OpenStrongman is an independent project and is not affiliated with any strongman federation, organization, or platform. We are not affiliated with OpenPowerlifting.org or OpenWeightlifting.org, though we share their belief that sports data should be open and freely accessible.
