Explore the Data

Build your own chart — pick a starting question or choose your own fields and filters below. Nothing here requires a spreadsheet, R, or Python. Every chart can be viewed as a plain table too.

Not sure where to start?
Line chart
Shows how a number changes across years. Use it for trends — like a world record climbing over decades.
Bar chart
Compares a number across categories side by side — like average finishing place by federation, or how many world records each event has produced.
Scatter plot
Shows every individual result as its own dot, instead of averaging them away. Best when you want to see the full spread, not just the summary.
