Federation & event websites
Strongman Corporation SC
The largest US amateur strongman federation. Events at all levels from local shows to national championships. Gateway to the Arnold amateur division. View upcoming SC events
United States Strongman USS
National amateur federation with events across the US and a pathway to national competition. Many USS events are hosted on Iron Podium. View upcoming USS events
Iron Podium IP
Competition management platform powering a large number of USS and independent events. The best place to find and register for upcoming amateur shows. Browse events on Iron Podium
Official Strongman Games OSG
Annual national championship open to all weight classes and divisions. One of the largest single-event strongman competitions in the US. Visit OSG website
Pro Strongman League PSL
Weight-class focused professional circuit with events livestreamed on YouTube. Events run through Iron Podium. Visit PSL website
World's Strongest Man WSM
The most prestigious international strongman competition, held annually since 1977. Athletes qualify through the Giants Live series. Visit WSM website
Giants Live GL
Official WSM qualifier series. Elite international arena events serving as the primary route to the World's Strongest Man competition. Visit Giants Live
Arnold Sports Festival ASC
Home of the Arnold Strongman Classic - an elite invitational held annually in Columbus, Ohio. Includes both professional and amateur divisions. Visit Arnold Sports
Rogue Invitational RI
Annual elite invitational hosted by Rogue Fitness. Features top international strongman and strongwoman athletes. Visit Rogue Invitational
Strongman Champions League SCL
International competition circuit with Grand Prix events throughout the year. Features top European and international professional athletes. Visit SCL website
Static Monsters SM
Global competition series held on the same weekend in 20+ countries. Athletes compete for max Log Press and max 18-inch Axle Deadlift. Top finishers qualify for the annual World Championships. Visit Static Monsters
Note: OpenStrongman is not affiliated with any of the federations or platforms listed above. Links are provided as a resource for athletes looking for upcoming competitions. Always check official federation websites for the most accurate event information.
